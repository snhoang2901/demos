CUSTOMER PROPOSAL TEMPLATE
Knowledge file for the "Ceco Proposal Builder" Copilot agent.

PURPOSE
The builder sends this proposal to their customer. It is built from a Ceco Customer Proposal Report (CPT)
exported from myPortal, plus the builder's own scope and prices. Executive readers should find the total,
the schedule and the decisions they need to make on page 1.

PLACEHOLDER CONVENTION
Anything not found in the CPT or given by the user appears in square brackets, for example
[Builder price - foundations] or [Not stated in CPT - confirm]. Never fill a placeholder with a guess.

LENGTH
Sections 1 to 7 should be about 5 pages. Technical detail goes in the Appendix.

COVER / LETTERHEAD

  PROPOSAL FOR: [Customer name]          PROJECT: [Project title from CPT]
  Date: [date]                           Valid until: [as stated in CPT, otherwise placeholder]

======================================================================
1. AT A GLANCE (must fit on one page)
  - Project and location
  - Prepared for / prepared by
  - Total investment: Ceco package (exactly as CPT, with page) + builder services (user-supplied) = total
  - What's included, in one sentence
  - Key dates (delivery / schedule) as stated
  - Decisions needed: [count], with a pointer to Section 5
  - Proposal validity

2. INVESTMENT
  Table: Line | Provided by | Amount | Source
    Ceco steel building package     | Ceco       | [exact CPT amount] | CPT p.[n]
    [Builder line, e.g. Foundations] | [Builder] | [user amount]      | Builder
    ...
    Total                            |            | [sum of the lines above, arithmetic shown]
  Payment terms, credit terms, taxes and freight: only as stated in the CPT or by the user; otherwise list
  them as open items.

3. SCOPE - WHO PROVIDES WHAT
  Three columns: Ceco provides | [Builder] provides | Not included
  - Ceco column: from the CPT only (building systems, wall/roof panels, accessories as listed).
  - Builder column: from the user only.
  - Not included: exclusions stated in the CPT or by the user. If none are stated, write
    "Exclusions not stated in CPT - confirm" and add it to Section 5.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES
  Table: Task | Ceco | [Builder] | Customer
  Typical rows to check (fill only from the CPT or user input; otherwise mark "confirm"):
  engineering and drawings, permits, site preparation, foundations/anchor bolts, delivery, unloading,
  erection, doors/windows, insulation, final inspection.

5. DECISIONS AND OPEN ITEMS
  Table: Item | What's missing or conflicting | Why it matters | Owner | Question for the customer
  Include: TBD values, blank fields, placeholder text such as "Enter a description here", missing loads,
  conflicting specifications, missing exclusions, missing terms.

6. YOUR BUILDING IN PLAIN LANGUAGE
  For each building or option: 3 to 6 short notes that say what the owner gets and why it matters.
  After each note show the original CPT wording and page in small print, for example:
    "Designed for the wind conditions required by the local building code."
    (CPT p.[n]: "[original wording]")
  Do not add performance claims that are not in the CPT.

7. ACCEPTANCE
  Customer signature / name / title / date
  [Builder] signature / name / title / date
  One line restating the total and the proposal validity.

======================================================================
APPENDIX - TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
  Consolidate the CPT technical sections once (codes and loads, deflections, geometry, sidewalls and
  endwalls, wall panels, accessories), labelled by building or option. Where values repeat, write
  "Same as Building 1". Keep CPT page references.

======================================================================
OTHER OUTPUT FORMATS

BUILDER REVIEW LIST (internal, before the customer sees anything)
  Item | CPT page | Severity (Blocker / Confirm / Nice to have) | Owner | Suggested fix or question

EXECUTIVE BRIEFING (for a president or CEO, one page)
  1. The decision being asked for
  2. Total investment and what it covers
  3. Schedule
  4. Top 3 risks or open decisions
  5. Next steps and who owns them
  Then offer a 5-slide outline:
  Slide 1 Project and goal | Slide 2 Investment | Slide 3 Scope and responsibilities |
  Slide 4 Decisions needed | Slide 5 Next steps

CUSTOMER COVER EMAIL (under 150 words)
  Purpose, total, the decisions needed (bulleted), the requested reply date, contact.
