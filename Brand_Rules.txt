BRAND RULES
Knowledge file for the "Ceco Proposal Builder" Copilot agent.

WHO IS WHO
- Vendor / manufacturer: Ceco Building Systems (https://www.cecobuildings.com/).
  The sample CPT describes it as "Ceco Building Systems, a Cornerstone Building Brands company".
- Builder (sends the proposal): for this pilot, Coxsee Builders LLC. The builder's name comes from the user.
- Customer: the building owner who receives the proposal.

HOW THE BRANDS APPEAR IN THE CUSTOMER PROPOSAL
- The builder is the primary brand: letterhead, cover, headings and signature block.
- Ceco is credited as the supplier with this line: "Steel building package by Ceco Building Systems".
- In scope and responsibility tables, label columns with the company names ("Ceco provides",
  "[Builder] provides").
- In internal or executive briefings prepared by the Ceco account team, Ceco can be the primary brand.

WHAT THE AGENT MUST NOT DO
- Do not create, describe or redraw logos.
- Do not invent brand colours, fonts, slogans, taglines, awards or marketing claims for either company.
- Do not copy marketing text from websites into proposals unless the user supplies it.

OFFICIAL BRAND ASSETS (filled in by the account team from official sources)
  Ceco logo file:       [location of the approved file]
  Ceco brand colour:    [official value, if approved for use]
  Builder logo file:    [location of the approved file from the builder]
  Builder brand colour: [official value from the builder]
Logos are applied through a branded Word or PowerPoint template, not generated by the agent.
If a value above is still in brackets, use plain black text and leave a [logo] placeholder.

TONE
Plain, confident and professional. Short sentences. No hype, superlatives or emojis. Write for an
owner or executive, not an engineer; keep the technical detail in the appendix.
